Machine Learning-Based Predictive Model for Hospital Readmission

Introduces RiskStack, an ensemble of logistic regression, random forest, gradient boosting, and neural networks for calibrated 30-day readmission risk. The paper studies probability calibration, decision-curve net benefit, risk tiers, and subgroup gaps using a synthetic cohort; it identifies multi-site validation as future work.

Ensembles · Calibration · Decision analysis
